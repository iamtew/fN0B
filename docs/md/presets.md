# Presets

The preset bar at the top shows the current preset. The arrows on either end step through the list, and clicking the middle opens it directly.

fN0B ships with three:

## Warm Drive

A soft-clip saturator followed by a gentle low-pass.

- Drive: 0 dB to +18 dB
- Cutoff: 20 kHz down to 4 kHz

Starts as a clean boost and ends warm and rounded. The filter closes as the drive comes up, so it never gets harsh. Good on guitars, synths, and vocals.

## Filter Funk

A resonant low-pass with drive riding along.

- Cutoff: 200 Hz to 8 kHz
- Resonance: 0.2 to 0.9
- Drive: 0 dB to +12 dB

A manual auto-wah. The resonance climbs as the filter opens, and a little warmth creeps in with it. Great on bass, keys, and anything that wants to talk.

## Bitcrush Destruct

Sample-rate reduction, then bit-depth reduction, then a soft clip.

- Sample rate: 1x to 16x reduction
- Bit depth: 16 to 4 bits
- Drive: 0 dB to +12 dB

Clean to digital grit to full destruction. The clip at the end keeps it from being painfully bright. Fun on drums and synths.
