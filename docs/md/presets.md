# Presets

The preset bar at the top shows the current preset. The arrows on either end step through the list, and clicking the middle opens it directly.

fN0B ships with six:

## Warm Drive

A soft-clip saturator followed by a gentle low-pass.

- Drive: 0 dB to +18 dB
- Cutoff: 20 kHz down to 4 kHz

Starts as a clean boost and ends warm and rounded. The filter closes as the drive comes up, so it never gets harsh. Good on guitars, synths, and vocals.

## Filter Funk

A resonant low-pass with drive riding along.

- Cutoff: 200 Hz to 8 kHz
- Resonance: 0.2 to 0.9
- Drive: 0 dB to +12 dB

A manual auto-wah. The resonance climbs as the filter opens, and a little warmth creeps in with it. Great on bass, keys, and anything that wants to talk.

## Bitcrush Destruct

Sample-rate reduction, then bit-depth reduction, then a soft clip.

- Sample rate: 1x to 16x reduction
- Bit depth: 16 to 4 bits
- Drive: 0 dB to +12 dB

Clean to digital grit to full destruction. The clip at the end keeps it from being painfully bright. Fun on drums and synths.

## Tape Echo

A delay line whose repeats pass through a saturator and a damper before feeding back, then a high-cut on the output.

- Delay: 60 to 450 ms
- Repeats: 15% to 60% feedback, saturated each pass
- Top end: 12 kHz down to 2.5 kHz

A turn stretches the repeats and each pass loses a little top and gains a little grit, the way tape chews a signal. Longer settings bend the pitch while you move the knob, which is the point. Good on vocals, synths, and anything that wants to trail off.

## Shimmer Verb

A reverb whose tail feeds a loop that replays it at double speed, so it comes back an octave up, then a tone filter.

- Room: 15% to 100%
- Wet: 10% to 45%
- Shimmer: 0% to 50% of the octave bloom
- Tone: 20 kHz down to 6 kHz

Starts as a small room and ends as a washed-out bloom with a rising octave sheen. The doubled tail is raw on purpose; that roughness is the character. Pad food.

## Phaser Sweep

A six-stage all-pass phaser with its own feedback, output trimmed so the notches stay musical.

- Sweep rate: 0.12 Hz to 3.5 Hz
- Depth: 20% to 100%
- Feedback: 10% to 70%

Slow breathing on chords, jet plane by the top of the knob. The trim keeps the comb peaks polite even with the feedback high.
